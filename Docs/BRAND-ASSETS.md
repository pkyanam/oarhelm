# Brand assets

Bundled marks identify Capy and the model providers. Capy artwork comes from its official brand asset pack. Some providers use icons from their official sites; the NVIDIA and Meta vector marks are from Simple Icons, whose artwork is CC0. Company trademarks remain with their owners.

- Capy mark and wordmark (light and dark): official [brand kit](https://capy.ai/_marketing/brand/capy-brand-kit.zip), linked from the context menu on [capy.ai](https://capy.ai/). Original PNGs retained.
- Anthropic: [official favicon](https://www.anthropic.com/images/icons/favicon-32x32.png); [legal and trademark guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal).
- Google Gemini: [official Gemini icon](https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_f94943af3be039176192d.png); [Google brand guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/).
- xAI: [official favicon](https://x.ai/favicon.ico), converted to PNG with sips; [xAI](https://x.ai/).
- DeepSeek: [official favicon](https://www.deepseek.com/favicon.ico), converted to PNG with sips; [DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com/).
- Qwen: [official site icon](https://img.alicdn.com/imgextra/i4/O1CN01OXv3EM1FN8t9W4P79_!!6000000000474-2-tps-80-80.png), linked by [qwen.ai](https://qwen.ai/).
- Tencent: [official site favicon](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/themes/tencent-web/assets/favicon/favicon-32x32.png); [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/).
- Xiaomi: [official site favicon](https://i02.appmifile.com/i18n/scm/favicon.png), linked by [mi.com](https://www.mi.com/).

- OpenAI: [logomark](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/agents_sdk/deployment_manager/frontend/src/openai-logomark.svg) from OpenAI's cookbook repository, converted to transparent PNG. The developer site favicon is a generic icon and was not used.
- Moonshot/Kimi: [Kimi favicon](https://www.kimi.com/favicon.ico), converted to PNG; represents the Kimi product brand.
- MiniMax: [official favicon](https://www.minimax.io/favicon.ico), converted to PNG.
- NVIDIA: [Simple Icons NVIDIA mark](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/nvidia.svg), converted to PNG. Simple Icons artwork is CC0; the trademark belongs to NVIDIA.
- Meta: [Simple Icons Meta mark](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/meta.svg), converted to PNG. Simple Icons artwork is CC0; the trademark belongs to Meta.
- Meituan: [official favicon](https://www.meituan.com/favicon.ico), converted to PNG.
- Particle AI: [official site icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6ace8efc702b317d7a00de14/6ace8efc702b317d7a00dfac_Webclip.png), linked in the metadata of [particle.ai](https://particle.ai/). Particle Network is a different company and its favicon is not shipped.

OpenAI, NVIDIA and Meta marks are rendered as templates to remain visible in light and dark appearances. Z.ai uses the [official organization avatar](https://github.com/zai-org.png?size=256) from its company-owned GitHub organization. The original [official SVG](https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-4.5/blob/main/resources/logo.svg) is retained in Design/BrandSources. Unknown providers use the neutral sparkles symbol.
